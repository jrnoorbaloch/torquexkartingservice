Image-first editorial card — full-bleed photo on top, tight title/body beneath, sharp 0px corners. The workhorse of range and benefit grids.

```jsx
<FeatureCard
  image="/models/812.jpg"
  eyebrow="The Range"
  title="812 Competizione"
  body="The most powerful V12 ever built."
  cta="Discover"
/>
```

`theme="dark"` (default) sits photo-flush on canvas with no border; `theme="light"` adds a hairline border and 32px padding for white editorial bands. Omitting `image` shows an atmospheric gradient placeholder — swap in real photography.
